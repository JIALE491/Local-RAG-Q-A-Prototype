# Store Opening and Closing Procedures

These steps apply to all three stores. Following them every day keeps people, cash and inventory safe.

## Opening the store

### Who opens?

Two employees open together, arriving 45 minutes before opening time, and one of them must be a manager or key holder. Never open alone: if your partner is late, wait in your car and call the store manager.

### Opening steps

1. Look for signs of a break-in before you unlock. If anything looks wrong, do not go in: call the police, then the on-call store manager.
2. Disarm the alarm with your personal code. Never share alarm codes; the alarm log shows whose code was used.
3. Count each register drawer. Every drawer starts the day with a $200 float. Report any difference over $5 to the store manager.
4. Turn on the lights and sign in to the point-of-sale system.
5. Check the rental desk for reservations due for pickup today.
6. Hold the daily team huddle 20 minutes before opening.

## Closing the store

### Who closes?

The two-person rule applies at closing too: at least two employees stay until the building is locked and the alarm is set.

### Closing steps

1. Make a final announcement 15 minutes before closing, and lock the doors at closing time once the last customer has left.
2. Two employees count each register together and sign the count sheet.
3. Put cash and checks in the deposit bag. The store safe may hold up to $2,000 overnight; anything above that goes to the bank's night deposit the same evening.
4. Walk the sales floor, fitting rooms and restrooms to make sure no one is left inside.
5. Set the alarm and lock up together, then walk to your cars together.

## Weather and emergency closures

The regional manager decides on weather closures by 6 a.m. Employees are notified through the scheduling app, and are paid for their scheduled shift if the store closes.
