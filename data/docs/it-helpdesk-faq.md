# IT Help Desk FAQ

How to get help with laptops, accounts, software and store systems.

## How do I contact the help desk?

Open a ticket in the IT portal, email helpdesk@tallowmere.example or call extension 4357 (H-E-L-P). The help desk is staffed from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Monday to Friday and from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Saturday, Pacific Time. Outside those hours, extension 4357 reaches the on-call technician, for urgent problems only.

## How do I reset my password?

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and confirm with your authenticator app. After 10 failed sign-in attempts your account locks for 30 minutes and then unlocks automatically; the help desk can unlock it sooner.

## How do I connect to the VPN?

Install the TrailLink VPN client from the IT portal and sign in with your work account. The VPN is required to reach internal systems, such as the Waypoint HR portal and the inventory tools, from outside the office.

## How do I get new software?

Request it in the IT portal. Software on the standard list is approved within 2 business days. Anything else needs a security review, which takes up to 10 business days.

## How long before a new hire's laptop is ready?

Managers should request a new hire's laptop and accounts at least 5 business days before the start date.

## When is my laptop replaced?

Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or sooner if they fail. The design team uses MacBook Pro laptops; everyone else gets a standard Windows laptop. Loaner laptops are available while yours is being repaired.

## A register is down in my store. Who do I call?

Call the store support line at extension 4400. A register that cannot take payments is a priority 1 issue, and a technician responds within 15 minutes, seven days a week, during store hours.

## Is there Wi-Fi for guests?

Yes. Guests at the head office and in the stores can use the Tallowmere-Guest network. Never connect a work laptop to it; work laptops connect automatically to the staff network.
