# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Store and distribution center jobs are on site. This policy covers head office roles, most of which are hybrid.

## How does hybrid work?

Hybrid employees work at the head office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, our anchor days, and choose where to work on the other days. Teams can agree on extra office days for planning sessions or launches. Everyone should be available for meetings during core hours, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Pacific Time.

## Can I work fully remotely?

Fully remote work is an exception. It needs approval from your department's vice president and the People Team, and it is reviewed every year. Fully remote employees come to the head office at least once a quarter, with travel paid under the Travel and Expense Policy.

## What equipment and allowances do I get?

- A company laptop, monitor and headset.
- A one-time home office stipend of $500 for things like a desk chair or a second monitor, available after 90 days of employment.
- An internet allowance of $40 per month, paid through payroll.

Equipment remains company property and must be returned within 10 days of your last day.

## Can I work from somewhere else for a while?

Within the United States, you can work from another location for up to 4 weeks per year with your manager's approval. Working from outside the United States is limited to 20 working days per year and needs approval from the People Team and IT at least 3 weeks in advance, because of tax and security rules. Working from countries under US sanctions is never allowed.

## What are the rules for working in public places?

Use the company VPN on any network that is not your home or the office, and keep customer and employee data off screens that others can see. The IT Security Policy has the full rules.
