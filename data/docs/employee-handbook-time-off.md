# Employee Handbook: Time Off

This chapter of the employee handbook covers vacation, sick leave, holidays and other paid time off. It applies to all employees; part-time employees earn vacation and sick leave in proportion to their scheduled hours. Parental leave has its own policy.

## Vacation

### How much vacation do I earn?

| Years of service | Vacation days per year |
|---|---|
| Less than 5 | 15 |
| 5 to 9 | 20 |
| 10 or more | 25 |

Vacation accrues every pay period from your first day, and you can use it as soon as it has accrued.

### Can I carry vacation over to next year?

You can carry up to 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, and any left after that date are forfeited. Unused vacation is not paid out, except where state law requires a payout when employment ends.

### How do I request vacation?

Request vacation in the Waypoint HR portal. For 3 or more consecutive days, submit the request at least 2 weeks ahead. Your manager approves or declines it within 3 business days.

### Are there times I can't take vacation?

Store and distribution center employees cannot take vacation between November 20 and January 2, our busiest season, unless their general manager approves an exception. Head office employees have no blackout period.

## Sick leave

Everyone gets 10 paid sick days per calendar year, for their own illness or to care for a family member. Sick days do not carry over. We only ask for a doctor's note after 3 or more consecutive sick days.

## Company holidays

We observe 10 paid holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve (half day) and Christmas Day. Store employees who work on a company holiday are paid 1.5 times their regular rate for the hours worked.

## Other paid time off

- Bereavement: 5 days for the death of an immediate family member, and 2 days for other relatives.
- Jury duty: full pay for up to 10 days of service per year.
- Voting: up to 2 hours of paid time to vote in public elections.
- Trail Day: 1 paid day each year to volunteer on a trail, river or conservation project.
