# Performance Review Process

We review performance twice a year, so that feedback is regular and nothing in the annual review comes as a surprise.

## When do reviews happen?

| Cycle | Month | Includes a rating |
|---|---|---|
| Spring check-in | April | No |
| Annual review | October | Yes |

Employees who are still in their 90-day introductory period when a cycle starts skip that cycle.

## How does a review work?

1. You write a self-review in the Waypoint HR portal. It is due 2 weeks before your review meeting.
2. Your manager writes their review and may ask up to three colleagues for input.
3. You and your manager meet to discuss both reviews and set goals for the next six months.

## What is the rating scale?

The annual review uses four ratings: Developing, Solid, Strong and Exceptional. Most employees who meet the expectations of their role are rated Solid. Before ratings are final, the managers in each department meet in calibration sessions to make sure the scale is applied consistently.

## How are pay increases decided?

Merit increases are based on the annual review rating and take effect on January 1. The leadership team sets the budget for increases each year. Promotions happen twice a year, in January and July, separately from merit increases.

## What if I disagree with my review?

You can add a written response to your review within 10 business days of the meeting. If you still disagree, ask the People Team to mediate a conversation with your manager.

## What is a performance improvement plan?

If performance falls well below expectations, your manager and the People Team may put a performance improvement plan in place. A plan lasts 60 days and sets clear goals, with check-ins every two weeks.

## What if I'm on leave during a review cycle?

Your review is postponed until you return. Being on leave never affects your rating.
