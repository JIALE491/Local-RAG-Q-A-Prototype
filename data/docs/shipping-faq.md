# Shipping FAQ

Answers to the questions customers ask most about delivery. For returns, see the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## What shipping options do you offer?

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | $6.95, free on orders of $75 or more |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $29.95 |

The $75 free-shipping threshold is measured after discounts and before tax. Delivery times start when the order ships, not when it is placed.

## When will my order ship?

Orders placed by 1 p.m. Pacific Time on a business day ship the same day from our Fenwick Yard distribution center. Orders placed later, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.

## Do you ship to Alaska, Hawaii and Canada?

Yes. Alaska and Hawaii get standard shipping only, which takes 7-10 business days and costs the same as standard shipping elsewhere. Shipping to Canada is a flat $19.95 and takes 7-12 business days; Canadian customs may charge duties and taxes on delivery, which the customer pays. We do not ship to other countries.

## How do kayaks, canoes and paddleboards ship?

Boats and boards ship by freight for a flat $89, in 7-14 business days, and free-shipping offers never apply to them. The freight company calls to schedule a delivery appointment, and delivery is to the curb. Someone 18 or older must be there to sign.

## Can I ship to a PO box?

Standard shipping can go to a PO box. Expedited, overnight and freight deliveries need a street address.

## Are there items you can only ship by ground?

Fuel canisters and bear spray are hazardous materials. They ship by ground within the contiguous United States only, so they cannot go to Alaska, Hawaii or Canada, and they cannot be sent expedited or overnight.

## Can I pick up my online order in a store?

Yes, for free. Choose a store at checkout and we email you when the order is ready, usually within 2 hours during store hours. Orders are held for 7 days, then canceled and refunded.

## My package says delivered, but I don't have it. What now?

Check with neighbors and around the delivery address, then contact customer service within 30 days of the expected delivery date. We open a claim with the carrier, which takes up to 10 business days, and then send a replacement or a refund.

## Can I change my shipping address after ordering?

Only before the order ships. Contact customer service as soon as possible: once a tracking number has been issued, we cannot redirect the package.
