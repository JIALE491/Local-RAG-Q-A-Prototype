# Gear Rental FAQ

Renting is the easiest way to try a new activity before you buy. Rentals are available at our Harrow Bay and Brindle Point stores; the Ashcombe Junction store does not offer rentals.

## What can I rent, and what does it cost?

| Item | Price per day |
|---|---|
| 2-person tent | $25 |
| 4-person tent | $35 |
| Sleeping bag | $12 |
| Sleeping pad | $6 |
| Bear canister | $5 |
| Touring kayak, with paddle and life jacket | $45 |
| Stand-up paddleboard, with paddle and leash | $40 |

Trailhead Rewards members get 10% off all rentals.

## How does weekend pricing work?

Pick up on Friday after 3 p.m. and return by noon on Monday, and you pay for 2 days instead of 3.

## Do I need to reserve, and how far ahead can I book?

Walk-ins are welcome when gear is available, but summer weekends sell out. You can reserve online up to 60 days before your pickup date.

## Is there a deposit?

We place a hold on a credit card when you pick up: $200 for kayaks and paddleboards, and $100 for all other gear. The hold is released when the gear comes back in good condition. Debit cards and cash are not accepted for the deposit.

## How old do I have to be?

Renters must be 18 or older and show a photo ID. Younger customers can use rented gear when a parent or guardian rents it and signs the rental agreement.

## What if I return gear late?

Each late day is charged at the daily rate. If gear is more than 3 days late and we cannot reach you, we treat it as unreturned and charge the full replacement cost to the card on file.

## Do I need to clean the gear?

Please return gear clean and dry. Tents and boats that come back muddy have a $20 cleaning fee, and tents returned wet have a $40 drying fee because of the risk of mildew.

## What happens if something gets damaged?

Normal wear is on us. Accidental damage is charged at our repair center rates, unless you bought the damage waiver: for $4 a day, it covers accidental damage up to $500 per rental. The waiver does not cover loss or theft.

## Can I cancel a reservation?

Cancel at least 48 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later cancellations, and reservations that are never picked up, are charged one day's rent.
