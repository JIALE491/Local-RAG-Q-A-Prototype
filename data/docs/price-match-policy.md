# Price Match Policy

We want you to pay a fair price without shopping around. If you find a lower price on an identical item, we match it, within the limits below.

## What qualifies for a price match?

The item must be identical: same brand, model, size and color. It must be in stock at the competitor and sold directly by a US retailer with a physical store or its own website. We check the price on the competitor's website, or in a current printed ad, before matching.

## What is excluded?

- Prices from third-party sellers on marketplace websites
- Membership or warehouse club prices
- Clearance, closeout, open-box, used or refurbished items
- Limited-quantity doorbuster deals on Black Friday and Cyber Monday
- Bundles, rebates, coupons and buy-one-get-one offers
- Prices that require signing up for a credit card or a loyalty program

## Can I get a price adjustment after I buy?

Yes. If we lower our own price, or you find a qualifying lower price elsewhere, within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference. Bring your receipt to a store, or send your order number through the price adjustment form on our website. For online orders, the 14 days count from the delivery date.

## Are there limits?

We match up to 2 units of the same item per customer. A price match cannot be combined with other promotions on the same item, but you still earn Trailhead Rewards points on the matched price.

## Does this apply to rentals and repairs?

No. Price matching applies only to new merchandise. Rental and repair prices are fixed.

## Who decides if a match is valid?

Store managers and customer service leads have the final say. They may decline a match if the competitor's price is an obvious error or the item cannot be verified.
