# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

Managers and new employees work through this checklist together. It runs from the week before the start date to the end of the introductory period.

## Before the first day (manager)

- Request a laptop and accounts through the IT portal. The IT Help Desk FAQ lists the lead time.
- Choose an onboarding buddy from the team: someone who is not the new employee's manager and has been with us for at least a year.
- Send the welcome email with the start time, the location and the dress code.

## First day

- Arrive at 9 a.m. Head office employees go to reception; store and distribution center employees go to their site manager.
- Have your ID badge photo taken.
- Pick up your laptop from IT and set up multi-factor authentication.
- Complete your tax forms and the I-9 employment eligibility form. Bring your identity documents: the I-9 must be completed within 3 business days of your start date.

## First two weeks

Finish the required training in the Waypoint HR portal within 14 days of your start date:

- Security awareness (45 minutes)
- Respectful workplace and harassment prevention (2 hours)
- Gear 101, an introduction to our products (90 minutes)

Store and distribution center employees also complete workplace safety training before their first shift on the sales floor or the warehouse floor.

## First 30 days

- Meet your manager one-on-one every week.
- Agree on goals for your first 30, 60 and 90 days.
- Meet your buddy at least twice a week. The buddy pairing lasts 60 days, and buddies get a $50 gift card when it ends.

## Day 90: end of the introductory period

The first 90 days are the introductory period. At the end of it, you and your manager have a check-in conversation about how things are going and what to focus on next.
