# Company Overview FAQ

Quick answers about who we are, where we operate and how to reach us. We share this page with new employees and with customers who ask about the company.

## When and where was Tallowmere Outfitters founded?

Tallowmere Outfitters was founded in 2009 by Maren Okafor and Teodor Lindqvist in Harrow Bay. The business started as a canoe and kayak rental shed on the north shore of Lake Wenmoor and opened its first store in Harrow Bay in 2011.

## What does the company sell?

We sell camping, hiking and paddling gear online and in our stores. About a third of sales come from our own brand, Tallowmere Trail, which covers backpacks, tents, sleeping bags and rain shells. We also rent gear at two of our stores; see the Gear Rental FAQ.

Online orders ship from the Fenwick Yard distribution center, which opened in 2019. The head office is at 400 Quarry Street, Harrow Bay.

## What are the store hours?

All three stores are open from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through Saturday, and from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday. Stores close at 4 p.m. on Christmas Eve, and are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

## How many people work here?

About 240 people as of January 2026: roughly 130 in the stores, 60 at the head office and 50 at the Fenwick Yard distribution center.

## What is the company's mission?

Get more people outside, with gear that lasts. In practice that means repairing before replacing, renting gear to people who are just starting out, and giving 1% of annual revenue to trail and river conservation groups.
