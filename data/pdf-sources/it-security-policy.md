## Purpose and scope

This policy protects customer, employee and company information. It applies to everyone who uses company systems or data, including contractors, on any device.

## Passwords

Use a passphrase of at least 14 characters for every work account, and never reuse a work password anywhere else. Passwords do not expire on a schedule; change yours right away if you think someone else may know it. Your last 10 passwords cannot be reused.

## Password manager

Store work passwords in Lockbox, the company password manager. Do not keep passwords in browsers, documents or notes.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the Waypoint HR portal, the VPN and every administrator account, including point-of-sale administration. Use the authenticator app or a hardware security key. Text message codes are not allowed, because they can be intercepted.

## Laptops and phones

- Company laptops have full-disk encryption, which must stay on.
- Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes without activity; lock yours whenever you step away.
- Install operating system updates within 14 days of release, and critical security patches within 72 hours.
- You can read work email on a personal phone only through the managed email app.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop, phone or security key to the IT Help Desk within 1 hour of noticing, at any time of day. IT can lock and erase the device remotely.

## Phishing

Report suspicious emails with the Report Phishing button in your email. Never sign in through a link in an email; go to the site directly instead. If you clicked a link or entered your password, tell IT immediately. Nobody gets in trouble for reporting a mistake.

## Payment card data

Card numbers are entered only into the point-of-sale system or the website checkout. Never write down, photograph, email or store a customer's card number anywhere.

## Removable media

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. IT can issue an encrypted USB drive when there is a business need.

## Training

Everyone completes security awareness training every year. New employees complete it as part of onboarding.

## Reporting security incidents

Report anything that might be a security incident to security@tallowmere.example or to the security on-call phone at extension 4911, which is answered 24 hours a day.

## Breaking this policy

Breaking this policy can lead to loss of system access and to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
