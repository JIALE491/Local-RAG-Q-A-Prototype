## Why we have a code

Our customers trust us to give honest advice, and we trust each other to do the right thing when no one is watching. This code describes what that means in practice. It applies to every employee, and to contractors and temporary staff while they work with us.

## Respect at work

We do not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status or any other protected characteristic. This applies at our sites, at company events, on business trips and online.

## Conflicts of interest

Avoid situations where your personal interests could influence your decisions at work. Tell your manager and the Ethics team about:

- A second job with a competitor, supplier or customer of Tallowmere Outfitters
- A financial interest of more than 1% in a competitor or supplier
- A family member or partner in your reporting line, or working for a supplier you deal with

Disclosing a conflict is not wrongdoing. Hiding one is.

## Gifts and entertainment

You can accept gifts worth up to $75 in total from any one supplier in a calendar year. Never accept cash, gift cards, or anything offered in exchange for a decision. Business meals are fine when there is a business purpose and they are not lavish. If you are offered something worth more than $75, decline it or report it to the Ethics team, who decide whether it can be kept, donated or returned.

## Supplier pro deals

Many brands offer our employees discounted pro deals. You can use them for gear for yourself and your household. Reselling gear bought through a pro deal is not allowed.

## Protecting information

Use customer and employee information only for the job you are doing, and never share it outside the company. Do not discuss unannounced products, sales results or company plans with anyone outside the company.

## Social media

You may say that you work here, but do not speak on behalf of the company unless Communications has asked you to. Never post customer information, or photos of customers without their permission.

## Raising concerns

If you see something that may break this code or the law, talk to your manager, the People Team or the Ethics team. You can also use the Ethics Line, which an independent company runs 24 hours a day at (360) 555-0199 and through a web form. Reports to the Ethics Line can be anonymous. The Ethics team starts looking into every report within 5 business days.

## No retaliation

We do not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or helps with an investigation. Retaliation is itself a serious breach of this code.

## Breaking the code

Breaking this code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and in some cases to legal action.

## Yearly acknowledgment

Every employee reads this code and confirms it in the Waypoint HR portal when they join, and again by January 31 every year.
