## Scope

This policy applies at our stores, the Fenwick Yard distribution center and the head office. Everyone is responsible for working safely and for stopping any work that looks unsafe.

## Reporting injuries and near misses

Report every injury, however small, and every near miss within 24 hours, using the incident form in the Waypoint HR portal or the paper form kept with each first aid kit. For a serious injury, call 911 first, then tell the manager on duty right away. Reports help us fix hazards before someone else gets hurt, and no one is ever disciplined for reporting.

## First aid

Every site has first aid kits and an automated external defibrillator (AED), and at least one trained first aider is scheduled on every shift. Their names are posted next to the time clock. Restock a first aid kit as soon as you use something from it.

## Lifting and carrying

Do not lift more than 50 pounds on your own; use a team lift or a cart. Kayaks and canoes are always carried by two people, even when they weigh less than 50 pounds, because of their length.

## Ladders

Keep three points of contact while climbing, and never stand on the top step of a stepladder. Lock rolling ladders before you climb them.

## Forklifts and powered equipment

Only certified operators may use forklifts and powered pallet jacks at the distribution center. Certification includes a practical test and must be renewed every 3 years. Inspect the equipment before every shift, and take it out of service if anything is wrong.

## Fuel canisters and other hazardous materials

Store fuel canisters in the locked flammable storage cabinet, never on open shelves in the back room. The sales floor may display no more than 48 canisters at a time. If a canister leaks, move people away, ventilate the area and call the store manager; do not try to stop the leak yourself.

## Heat and cold at the distribution center

When the temperature in the warehouse goes above 85 degrees Fahrenheit, everyone takes a 10-minute cool-down break every hour, and drinking water is provided. In winter, the loading dock doors stay closed except while trucks are loading.

## Safety committee

The safety committee meets monthly, with at least one representative from every site. Anyone can raise a concern with their representative or directly at a meeting.

## Returning to work after an injury

After a work injury, we offer modified duties that fit your doctor's restrictions, so you can return to work while you recover. The People Team coordinates with you, your manager and your doctor.
