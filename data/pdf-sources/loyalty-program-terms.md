## About the program

Trailhead Rewards is our free loyalty program. Anyone 18 or older with a US or Canadian mailing address can join online, in a store or in our app. Each person can have one account, and points cannot be transferred or combined between accounts.

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on merchandise, after discounts and before tax and shipping. Tallowmere Trail products earn 2 points per $1. Gift card purchases, rentals, repairs and shipping charges do not earn points. Points appear in your account within 48 hours of a purchase.

## Reward certificates

Every 200 points automatically turn into a $10 reward certificate, sent by email. Certificates can be used in stores and online, and expire 90 days after they are issued. They have no cash value and cannot be used to buy gift cards.

## Point expiration

Points do not expire as long as you make at least one purchase every 18 months. If an account has no purchases for 18 months, all of its points expire.

## Membership tiers

Tiers are based on what you spend in a calendar year:

- Base: every member, from the day they join
- Ridge: $500 or more in a year. Free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum.
- Summit: $1,500 or more in a year. Free expedited shipping, early access to sales, and an extended return window (see the Returns and Refunds Policy).

When you reach a tier, you keep it for the rest of that year and all of the following year.

## Birthday reward

Members get a $5 birthday certificate during their birthday month. Add your birthday to your profile at least 30 days before your birthday month to receive it that year.

## Returns and points

When you return an item, the points it earned are taken back. If you paid partly with a reward certificate, the refund covers only the amount you paid by other means, and the certificate value goes back to your account if the certificate has not expired.

## Member pricing on rentals

Members get a discount on gear rentals. The Gear Rental FAQ has the details.

## Changes to the program

We may change these terms or end the program with 30 days' notice by email. Points and certificates you already have stay valid until their normal expiration date.
