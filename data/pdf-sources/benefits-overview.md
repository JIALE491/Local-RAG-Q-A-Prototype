## Who is eligible

Full-time employees, scheduled for 30 or more hours a week, are eligible for all benefits on the first day of the month after their start date. Part-time employees scheduled for 20 to 29 hours a week are eligible for dental, vision, the 401(k) plan and the employee discount.

## Medical plans

You can choose between two medical plans:

- Meadow PPO: a traditional plan with a $750 deductible for individual coverage. The company pays 85% of the premium for employee coverage and 70% for dependents.
- Alpine HDHP: a high-deductible plan with a health savings account. The company pays 95% of the premium for employee coverage and 80% for dependents, and puts $750 a year into your health savings account, or $1,500 if you cover dependents.

## Dental and vision

The company pays 100% of the premium for employee-only dental and vision coverage. You pay the difference for dependent coverage.

## Saving for retirement {bookmark: 401(k) plan}

You can join the 401(k) plan after 60 days of employment. The company matches 100% of the first 4% of pay that you contribute. Company matching contributions vest over 3 years, one third each year; your own contributions are always yours.

## Insurance

- Life insurance: coverage of 1 times your annual salary, at no cost to you. You can buy more for yourself and your family.
- Short-term disability: 60% of your pay for up to 12 weeks, starting after a 7-day waiting period, at no cost to you.
- Long-term disability: 60% of your pay, starting after 90 days of disability, at no cost to you.

## Employee discount

From your first day, you get 30% off regular-price merchandise and 40% off Tallowmere Trail products, in stores and online. The discount does not apply to gift cards, rentals or items already on sale, and it is capped at $3,000 of retail value per calendar year. It is for you and your household only.

## Wellness stipend

Every employee gets $300 a year for things that keep them active, such as a gym membership, race entry fees or fitness classes. Claim it through the expense app by December 15; unused amounts do not carry over.

## Employee assistance program

The employee assistance program offers free, confidential counseling for you and your household, up to 6 sessions per issue, plus legal and financial advice lines. It is available 24 hours a day.

## Open enrollment and life events

You choose or change benefits during open enrollment, which runs from November 1 to 15 each year, for coverage starting January 1. After a qualifying life event, such as marriage, a birth or adoption, or losing other coverage, you have 30 days to make changes.
