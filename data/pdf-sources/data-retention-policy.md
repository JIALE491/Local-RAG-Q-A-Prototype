## Why we keep and delete data

We keep information only as long as we need it for the business or the law requires it, and then we delete it securely. Keeping data longer than necessary increases the harm if it is ever exposed.

## Retention periods

The list below shows how long we keep the main types of records, and when each period starts.

- Customer order and payment records: 7 years from the order date
- Customer accounts with no activity: deleted 3 years after the last sign-in or purchase
- Marketing email consent records: while the customer is subscribed, plus 2 years
- Store security camera footage: 30 days, unless it is needed for an incident
- Applications from people we did not hire: 1 year after the position closes, or 2 years if the applicant agreed to join our talent pool
- Employee personnel files and payroll records: 7 years after employment ends
- Email: 3 years from the date it was sent or received
- System backups: 35 days

## Payment card numbers

We never store full card numbers. Our payment provider replaces them with tokens at the point of sale and at checkout, so card numbers never reach our own systems.

## Customer requests to delete data

Customers can ask us to delete their personal data through the privacy form on our website or by calling customer service. We confirm the person's identity first, then complete the deletion within 30 days. Order records that we must keep for tax purposes are kept until their retention period ends, but they are removed from marketing lists and customer profiles.

## Legal holds

When a lawsuit, investigation or audit is expected, Legal can place a hold on related records. A legal hold overrides every retention period in this policy until Legal lifts it. Employees are told when records they manage are under a hold.

## Secure deletion

Paper records are shredded on site. Laptops and storage devices are wiped to industry standards before they are reused, or physically destroyed by a certified vendor.
