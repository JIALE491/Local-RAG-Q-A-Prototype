## Overview

This policy explains how customers return or exchange items bought from Tallowmere Outfitters in our stores or online, and how and when refunds are paid. It applies to all three stores and to orders shipped from our distribution center. Gear rentals have their own terms in the Gear Rental FAQ.

## Return window

Unused items can be returned within 45 days of purchase for store purchases, or within 45 days of delivery for online orders. Trailhead Rewards members in the Summit tier have 90 days. Bring the receipt or order number; returns without one are covered further down.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, with tags attached, and in the original packaging where possible. Footwear must be clean and show no outdoor wear. We inspect every return. Used items are accepted only when they are defective, and then the Warranty and Repairs Policy applies instead of this policy.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Rope and cord cut to a custom length
- Fuel canisters that have been opened
- Personalized or engraved items
- Gift cards
- Items marked Final Sale
- Hydration bladders, mouthpieces and other hygiene items once their packaging is opened

## How to return an item

In a store: bring the item to any of our three stores. Store returns are processed at the counter, and you leave with a receipt that shows the refund.

By mail: start a return from your order page to get a prepaid return label. The label costs $7.50, which is deducted from your refund. Labels are free for exchanges and for items that arrived damaged or wrong.

Boats and boards: kayaks, canoes and paddleboards can be returned in any store, or collected by our freight partner for $89.

## When refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. For mail returns, we issue the refund within 5 business days after the package arrives at our distribution center. For store returns, we issue it at the counter. Card issuers can take another 3 to 10 business days to show a refund on your statement. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is caused by our mistake.

## Refunds by payment method

- Credit and debit cards: refunded to the same card.
- Gift cards: refunded to a new gift card, which is emailed to you for online orders.
- Cash: refunds up to $100 are paid in cash at the counter. Larger cash refunds are mailed as a check within 10 business days.
- Pay-in-installments services: refunded to the installment provider, which then adjusts your payment plan.
- Split payments: each payment method is refunded in proportion to the amount it paid.

## Holiday returns

Items bought between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year, even when the usual 45-day window would end earlier. The holiday window never shortens the 90-day window for Summit members.

## Returns without a receipt

Without a receipt or order number, we offer store credit for the lowest price the item sold for in the last 60 days. A government photo ID is required, and each customer can make up to 3 returns without a receipt in any 12-month period.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color are free. For mail exchanges, we ship the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your return package, so you do not have to wait for it to reach us.

## Gifts

With a gift receipt, the recipient gets store credit, or can ask us to refund the person who bought the gift. Gift returns use the same return window, counted from the purchase date on the gift receipt.

## Damaged or wrong items

Tell us within 14 days of delivery if an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered. Send a photo through your order page or to help@tallowmere.example, and we ship a replacement at no cost, usually within 2 business days.
