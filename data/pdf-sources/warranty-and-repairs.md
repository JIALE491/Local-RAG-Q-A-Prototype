## Our promise

We stand behind what we sell. If gear fails because of a defect in materials or workmanship, we repair it, replace it or give you credit, as described below. Repairing first keeps gear on the trail and out of landfills.

## What is covered

Tallowmere Trail products, our own brand, are covered for the lifetime of the product against defects in materials and workmanship. Electronics such as headlamps, GPS units and power banks are covered for 2 years from the purchase date, whatever the brand. Other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty; during the first year after purchase, we handle the claim with the manufacturer for you.

## What is not covered

- Normal wear and tear, such as worn fabric, faded colors or flattened insulation
- Damage from accidents, misuse, animals, fire or improper care
- Damage from long exposure to sunlight
- Products used commercially or rented out to others
- Alterations or repairs made by anyone other than our repair center

## How to make a warranty claim

Submit a claim through the warranty form on our website or at any store. Include your order number or receipt, and photos that show the problem. We review claims within 5 business days. If we need to see the item, we send a free shipping label, or you can drop it off at a store.

## What happens next

We try to repair the item first. If it cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same product or the closest current model. If the product has been discontinued and nothing comparable is available, we give store credit for the price you paid.

## Paid repairs

The repair center at our Harrow Bay store also repairs gear that is out of warranty or damaged by accident, whether or not you bought it from us. Typical prices:

- Zipper slider replacement: $18
- Seam repair: from $25
- Tent pole section replacement: $12 per section
- Patch for a sleeping pad or inflatable kayak: $15

Repairs usually take 10 to 15 business days. We give you a quote before starting any paid repair over $50.
